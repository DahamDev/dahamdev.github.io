About Me

A little about me, on and off the trail.

I'm a software and cloud engineer focused on understanding how systems behave and making them reliable. I currently work at Amazon Web Services as a Cloud Support Engineer specializing in Kubernetes and container services. My background includes Java, Spring Boot, Python, AWS, EKS, ECS, infrastructure as code, observability, and production incident analysis.

Away from the keyboard, I like getting out into nature and exploring New Zealand on foot. Hiking gives me time to slow down, enjoy the view, and be curious about something other than code.

This website brings those parts of my life together: technical work, notes from things I’m learning, and room for the interests that take me outdoors.
